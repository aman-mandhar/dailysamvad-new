# Editorial permission matrix

Reporter permissions cover own drafts, submission/resubmission, own status, and relevant history. Reviewer permissions cover assigned posts, starting review, corrections, approval/rejection, and history; reviewers do not publish. Editors/admins can view all, assign/reassign, review, approve/reject, schedule, publish, archive, and restore. Authorization is permission-, ownership-, assignment-, active-account-, and status-driven rather than role-name-driven. SEO/media/subscriber permissions do not grant workflow transitions.

Phase 2.1-C adds `assign reviewers` and `view workflow history` without deleting custom role permissions or direct user permissions. Inactive accounts fail policy checks.
